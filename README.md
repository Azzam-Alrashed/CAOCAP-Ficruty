# CAOCAP 🪐 كوكب

**An end-to-end command center for AI agents and computers.**

CAOCAP is building a new way to control computing: express your intent, let agents coordinate the work, watch it happen, and redirect them from your iPhone or iPad.

## Why It Exists

Hi ✋🏼, I'm Azzam Alrashed. I don't know what I'm doing—and that is part of the fun. CAOCAP is my passion project and my way of exploring what computing could become.

My development journey started when a freelancer asked for more money to continue building my simple app. I was broke, so in 2018—with only two years of experience—I decided to build it myself.

The original goal was to make turning an **idea into software** easy, simple, cheap, and fun. That goal has grown into something more ambitious.

**CAOCAP refuses to accept today's way of controlling computers as the final form.**

## The New Direction

CAOCAP is becoming an end-to-end command center for intelligent computing.

Instead of operating every computer yourself, you direct the intelligence operating them. CAOCAP turns your intent into coordinated work across agents, machines, and environments.

The product is not defined by one interface, agent, model, or computer. It is a control layer that makes intelligent computing visible, understandable, and steerable.

This direction is still being built. The current iOS app is its foundation, not the finished product.

The canonical product direction is documented in [CAOCAP Product Direction](docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md).

## How It Works

The iOS app is a conversational mission-control whiteboard. Tell CoCaptain or CoStar what you want, and your CoPilot creates, explains, and modifies the multi-agent workflow for you.

The workflow stays visible on the canvas. Select any agent, task, or connection to ask what it does or request a change. You never need to wire the workflow manually.

Work runs on your connected Mac, Linux, or Windows computers—or on computers hosted by CAOCAP. Personal and cloud computers can participate in the same mission.

A lightweight CAOCAP Runner connects each computer and executes the assigned agent work. Its screen can stream to the iOS app, letting you observe progress and redirect the agent through your CoPilot.

Each Runner gives its agent a face: a simple animated 2D shape with a unique gradient and two dot eyes. Small expressions make the agent's identity and working state easy to understand.

## The Loop

**Command → orchestrate → execute → observe → redirect.**

You give the mission. Your CoPilot builds the orchestration. Agents execute it across available computers. You watch the work and redirect it through conversation.
